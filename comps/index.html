Rank and File: design comps

Five starting points. Each page shows the map and the strategy card with the same sample data and the current board colours; only the design changes. These are static renderings, not wired to the app.

  1. 01ScoresheetA tournament scoresheet: cream paper, ruled lines, typewriter notation, tally marks and a red stamp.
  2. 02Field manualLeans into the name and the missions: khaki and olive, a survey grid lettered like a board, one signal orange.
  3. 03Chess columnThe newspaper chess column: no boxes, diagrams with captions, big serif headlines, nearly black and white.
  4. 04TransitOpenings as stations on a line diagram: hollow lines for White moves, solid for Black, signage type and yellow.
  5. 05SlateDark, with the board's two slates as the whole palette, the map over a giant chessboard, poster-scale type.
