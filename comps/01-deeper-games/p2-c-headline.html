C · The headline figure

The score against stronger players is set as large as the reach figure above it. The three bands appear as bars underneath.

How it fits
  • It uses patterns the box already has: a large figure with a sentence under it, and bars like Where the other 75 went.
  • The bars show score only. Won, Drawn and Lost for each band move to the tooltip, so the box gets less detail than A or B.
  • Two large figures in one box compete for attention. The red one wins, which is the point, but only when the player is struggling.
