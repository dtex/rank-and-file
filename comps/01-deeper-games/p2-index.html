Phase 2 of 01-deeper-games-and-rating-stats.md. Three places for the table on an opening's page, each with its own marker in the outline.

The design doc puts a six-column table "next to Your record here". That box is 290 pixels wide, which fits about four narrow columns, so the doc's table can't go there as written. A keeps the table in the box with fewer columns. B gives it a full-width section of its own. C leads with the one number the work exists for: how the player scores against stronger opponents.

Every frame uses the same player: 45 Sicilian games as Black, 9 of them against opponents rated more than 100 above them, who scored 22%. That makes the opening a struggle.
