A · In the record box

The bands go straight under Won, Drawn and Lost, so the record and its breakdown are read together.

How it fits
  • Four columns fit in the box: Opponents, Games, W–D–L and Score. Won, Drawn and Lost share one column as 10–1–3, the way results are written in tournament tables.
  • The band names are short: Weaker, Your level and Stronger. One line of small print explains the 100 points.
  • When the player is struggling (8 or more games against stronger players, scoring under 35%), the Stronger row turns red.
  • At widths below 980 pixels the box already moves under the board and ideas, and the table goes with it.
