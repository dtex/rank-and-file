B · By colour

Two contents lists, White's openings and Black's, each sorted by where the mission stands. The tree is left to the map.

This one drops the tree. It answers a different question from the outline: not how do these openings connect but what am I working on, and what have I finished. Each opening's line of moves is printed at the end of its row, so the connection can still be read, but nothing is nested.

Unexplored lines become one sentence at the foot of each column.

1On a laptop

The active mission is set larger than the rest. Parked and complete openings follow under their own labels.

2On a phone

What works

  • The shortest and calmest of the four. Sixteen openings fit on one laptop screen without scrolling.
  • Matches how missions work: one active opening for each colour, with parked ones beside it.
  • Stacks cleanly on a phone, White first, then Black.

What it costs

  • Says nothing about which opening leads to which, except through the moves. Players would switch to the map for that.
  • The active missions appear twice on a laptop, here and in the sidebar.
  • Unexplored lines lose their place: the count is for the colour, not for an opening.

What it needs

  • A new component in place of cb-outline. Nothing new from the server.
  • A decision on the sidebar: either accept the repeat, or let the parked openings' Resume buttons move into this list.
