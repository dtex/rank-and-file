Instead of the outline

Four ways to show the openings as something other than a folding list, for the second layout of the Openings screen.

The outline is the layout beside the map, and the one a phone is likely to keep. It shows depth by indenting, hides most openings behind arrows until they are unfolded, and gives every unexplored line a row of its own, so a player with sixteen openings also scrolls past twenty-five rows that say unexplored.

Each option below replaces it, and keeps the map as the other layout. All four use the same sample player: sixteen openings uncovered, one active mission for each colour, two parked, and twenty-five lines still unexplored.

  1. aLines SuggestedA table with a row for every opening and a column for every turn, like a chess book's index of variations. Keeps the whole tree in view and can be filtered.
  2. bBy colourTwo contents lists, White's openings and Black's, sorted by where the mission stands. The shortest, and the only one that gives up the tree.
  3. cDiagramsThe map's tiles set in rows under the reply they grow from. The most visual and the tallest.
  4. dStep throughOne position at a time, with the moves that leave it listed beneath the board. The best on a phone, with no overview.
The same in every option
  • The masthead, the sidebar and the map are unchanged. Only the second layout and its name on the switch differ.
  • None needs anything new from the server. Each is a component in public/src/openings.js in place of cb-outline.
  • Accounts that saved outline as their layout need that value kept or mapped to the new one, in routes/settings.js and services/store.js.
  • Unexplored lines are counted instead of listed, in all four.
  • The layout is saved for each kind of device, so two could ship: A on laptops and D on phones is the pairing I would try first.
  • D is live in its frames, and so are A's filters. Other links go nowhere.
